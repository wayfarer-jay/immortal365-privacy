IMMORTAL 365 Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026. Version 1.

Contact: jk2flow@gmail.com

IMMORTAL 365 ("the App") is a fitness, nutrition, recovery and mind-training app. It has no user accounts and the Developer runs no servers that receive your data. Everything you record is stored on your iPhone. See also the Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use.

What the App stores on your device

Workouts, food logs, body measurements, habits, breathing, meditation, sauna and cold sessions, eating-window records, sleep and recovery summaries, awards, your goals, chosen path and settings. This data stays on your device. You can export or delete it under Hub › Data & backup. The Developer does not receive, sell, rent, or share it, does not show ads, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Apple Health

If you allow it, the App reads steps, distance, energy, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, VO2 max, respiratory rate, oxygen saturation, wrist temperature, sleep, weight, body fat, lean mass, height, date of birth, sex, water and workouts from Apple Health to show your trends. If you turn it on, the App writes completed workouts, weigh-ins, water and active energy back. Health data is not used for advertising or marketing and is not sent to the Developer.

Location

Location is used only while you run a live cardio session, to measure distance and draw the route, which stays on your device. If you turn on Weather, your location rounded to about 1 km is sent to Open-Meteo once a day to get the forecast.

Camera and photos

The camera scans food barcodes; images are not recorded or uploaded. If you use the optional AI meal photo log, the photo you choose is sent as described below.

Features that use the internet

  • Food search: search words or a scanned barcode go to USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts.
  • Weather (optional): rounded location goes to Open-Meteo.
  • AI coach and photo log (optional, off by default): if you add your own API key and accept the in-app consent, the text or photo and the context you choose go to Anthropic's API. Your key is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. Anthropic's handling of that data is described in Anthropic's own policy.

Those services receive only what the request needs and are not given your name, because the App has no account. They have their own privacy policies. You can avoid all of them by not using those features.

Notifications

Local reminders (rest timers, habits, eating-window nudges, streak nudge) are scheduled on your device. No push notification server is used.

Security

Data stays in the App's protected storage on your device and your API key in the Keychain. No system is perfectly secure and the Developer cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your device locked and updated and keep backups.

Retention and your choices

Data stays until you delete it. You can revoke Health, location, camera and notification permissions in iOS Settings, delete all App data under Hub › Data & backup, or delete the App. Because the Developer holds no copy of your data, the Developer cannot retrieve or restore it for you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California, Washington and other U.S. states, the EU and UK), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your data and to withdraw consent. Because your data is on your device and under your control, you can exercise these yourself in the App. For anything else, contact jk2flow@gmail.com.

Age

The App is for adults 18 and older and is not directed to children.

Not medical advice

The App shows estimates and general information, is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. See the Terms of Use.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when this policy changes, and ask for acceptance in the App where required.
