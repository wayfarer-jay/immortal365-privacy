# IMMORTAL 365 Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026. Version 1.
Contact: jk2flow@gmail.com

This policy supplements the Privacy Policy and is provided for state consumer health data laws, including Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar laws. In short: your health data stays on your device and the Developer does not receive it.

## Categories of consumer health data the App handles
Body measurements and composition (weight, body fat, lean mass, height), activity and workouts, heart rate and heart rate variability, sleep, respiratory rate and oxygen saturation, wrist temperature, nutrition and water logs, eating-window records, goals and training choices, breathing, meditation, sauna and cold session records, and readiness and recovery scores calculated on your device.

## Purposes
Only to show you your own trends, targets and plans inside the App, and, if you turn them on, to save workouts and weigh-ins back to Apple Health and to send data to services you choose.

## Sources
You, Apple Health and the devices and apps that write to Apple Health, the sensors on your device, and any file you import.

## Where it is held
On your device. The Developer does not collect, receive, store, sell or license your consumer health data and does not hold it on any server.

## Sharing
The Developer does not share your consumer health data. When you use these optional features, the data needed for the request goes to the named service: Anthropic (AI coach and photo log, only after your consent and with your own key), USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts (food search words and barcodes), Open-Meteo (rounded location for weather, only if you turn it on). The App has no advertising or analytics SDKs.

## Consent and withdrawal
Each Health data type is requested separately by Apple Health and each optional online feature is off until you turn it on. You can withdraw consent at any time in iOS Settings › Health › Data Access and in the App's settings, and by deleting your data under Hub › Data & backup or deleting the App.

## Your rights
You may confirm whether the App holds your data, access it, withdraw consent, and delete it. You can do all of these directly in the App and on your device. For help or to make a request, email jk2flow@gmail.com. If you disagree with a decision you may appeal by emailing the same address, and you may contact your state attorney general.

## Geofencing
The App does not use geofencing around any health care facility.
