# IMMORTAL 365 Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026
Contact: jk2flow@gmail.com

IMMORTAL 365 ("the app") is a fitness, nutrition, recovery and mind-training app. It has no accounts and no servers of its own. Everything you record is stored on your iPhone.

## What the app stores on your device
Workouts, food logs, body measurements, habits, breathing, meditation, sauna and cold sessions, sleep and recovery summaries, awards, your goals and settings. This data stays on your device. You can export or delete it at any time under Hub › Data & backup. The app does not sell data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

## Apple Health
If you allow it, the app reads steps, distance, energy, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, VO2 max, respiratory rate, oxygen saturation, wrist temperature, sleep, weight, body fat, lean mass, height, date of birth, sex, water and workouts from Apple Health to show your trends. If you turn it on, it writes completed workouts, weigh-ins, water and active energy back. Health data is not sent to us, is not used for advertising, and is not shared with third parties except as described under "Features that use the internet" and only when you use them.

## Location
Location is used only while you run a live cardio session, to measure distance and draw the route, which stays on your device. If you turn on Weather, your location rounded to about 1 km is sent to Open-Meteo once a day to get the forecast.

## Camera and photos
The camera scans food barcodes; images are not recorded or uploaded. If you use the optional AI meal photo log, the photo you choose is sent as described below.

## Features that use the internet
- **Food search:** your search words or a scanned barcode are sent to the USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts to look up nutrition data.
- **Weather (optional):** rounded location is sent to Open-Meteo.
- **AI coach and photo log (optional, off by default):** if you add your own Anthropic API key and accept the in-app consent, the text or photo and the context you choose are sent to Anthropic's API to produce a reply. Your key is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. Anthropic's handling of that data is described in Anthropic's own policy.
These services receive only what is needed for the request and are not given your name or account, because there is no account.

## Notifications
Local reminders (rest timers, habits, eating-window nudges, streak nudge) are scheduled on your device. No push notification server is used.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13.

## Health information, not medical advice
The app shows estimates and general information. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Breathing, cold and sauna features carry safety warnings; talk to a clinician before starting if you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or take medication.

## Your choices
You can revoke Health, location, camera and notification permissions in iOS Settings at any time, delete all app data under Hub › Data & backup, or delete the app.

## Changes
We will update this page and its date if this policy changes.
